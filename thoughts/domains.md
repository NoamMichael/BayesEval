# Domains for BayesEval

## Finance

We will challenge models to choose a company from the S&P 500 based off of some information. We will provide information such as:

* Industry (Tech, Food & Beverage, CPG, etc.)
* PE Ratio range
* Market cap range
* Revenue range

We will compare the model's confidence with the amount of companies that fit the description. For example, if there are 5 companies that satisfy the criteria then the probability would be $1/5$ or $20\%$.
